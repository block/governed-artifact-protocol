These fictional stories explore how GAP could help when content passes from one person or tool to another. The teams and situations are made up to illustrate the checks involved; they do not report real deployments or measured results.

Each story focuses on one handoff and the question it raises for the receiving tool. It follows that question through a release check and the team’s next decision. You can read them without running a demo or knowing the record format.
